Frequently asked questions

How much does a Ganpati invite website cost?

₹99, one time, for unlimited guests. With 18% GST that comes to ₹117 at checkout. Pay via UPI, submit your UTR, and we unlock your editor after confirming the payment.

What is a Ganpati invite website?

It is the modern form of a digital Ganpati invitation. Instead of a card image or a PDF you get a real website at your own link - animated, with music and a grand reveal - that guests walk through on their phone to see your date, venue, live map, aarti schedule and RSVP. You share it as one link on WhatsApp.

How do I get my invite website and how long does it take?

Choose a design, pay ₹99 + GST via UPI, enter your UTR, and keep the page open. After we confirm the payment, your editor opens automatically — usually within a few minutes.

Can I get my invite in Hindi or English?

Yes - every design comes in Marathi, Hindi and English. Pick the language above the designs and the preview changes instantly; your invite is then created in that language, and you can switch it later in the editor. Whatever you type yourself stays exactly as you type it, in any language or script.

Do I have to message or call anyone to order?

No - and that's the point. There is no WhatsApp chat and no phone call anywhere in the process. You pay on this page, the editor opens by itself, you type your details and press publish. Nobody has to reply to you first.

Can I change the details after I have shared my invite link?

Yes. Open your private edit link any time, change the venue, timing or photos, and press publish again. The invite link your guests already have stays the same and shows the new details instantly - no need to resend anything.

How long does my invite website stay online?

Your invite link stays live through the full festival and well beyond - at minimum until 31 March of the following year, so you can keep it as a memory.

Can I share it on WhatsApp?

Yes - that is what it is built for. It is one link that previews with your family name, date and venue before anyone even taps, and it forwards from group to group.

I am bringing Bappa home outside India - does this work?

Yes. It is a website, so it opens exactly the same in Dubai, London, Singapore, Sydney or New Jersey. Payment is in ₹ (INR) and works with international cards. This is often the only way relatives across countries get a proper invitation at all.
