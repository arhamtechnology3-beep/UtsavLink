The Sacred Ceremonies · पवित्र सोहळे
10:00 Morning
Sakharpuda
साखरपुडा · Engagement Ceremony

The sacred exchange of sugar — sweetening the bond between two families. Rings are exchanged, blessings are given, and a joyous feast marks the beginning of this union.

09:00 Day Before
Halad Chadhavane
हळद चढवणे · Turmeric Ceremony

Turmeric paste — the sacred Halad — applied by family members to both bride and groom. It purifies, protects, and bestows the golden glow before the wedding day arrives.

11:23 Wedding Day
Lagna Muhurtha
लग्न मुहूर्त · The Sacred Wedding

The eternal Saptapadi — seven steps, seven vows, before Agni the sacred fire. The Mangalsutra is tied, sindoor applied. Two souls become one under the blessings of Ganesh and Lakshmi.

07:00 Evening
Rukhwat & Reception
रुखवत · The Grand Feast

The Wada courtyard fills with music, laughter, and a traditional Maharashtrian feast. Puranpoli, Modak, and fine cuisine. Live Dhol-Tasha ensemble fills the night air.
